"Intermittent" is a statement about the RATE, not a diagnosis: model nondeterminism is the usual cause and it is not the only one. A trial can also read as held because the break happened somewhere the judge was not looking — a secretbot chain printed the recovery code in turn one of all three trials, refused in turn two, and published 2/3 until every turn was judged.
